### Verification details and limitations

- The municipality's [mobility page](https://www.sofia.bg/transport-data) links both its own data portal and the operator announcement, and describes static GTFS as routes, stops and schedules. The announcement embeds its article in the HTML `data-page` JSON; checking only rendered text without JavaScript can miss the endpoint and terms links.
- The GTFS archive was inspected in memory for feed metadata only. Its ZIP entries for `feed_info.txt` and `calendar_dates.txt` carry 2026-10-05 modification dates (ZIP timezone unspecified); `feed_start_date` is the stronger explicit validity-date evidence. No timetable rows or contact fields are reproduced here. No `Last-Modified` HTTP header was supplied. The catalog's licence mismatch must be resolved with the publisher before choosing terms for real data redistribution.
- The municipal layer is a district-boundary spatial dataset, not a park/amenity inventory. Its as-of date comes from the publisher, not an inference from the upload timestamp. Its generic CC BY label does not establish a specific licence version. The municipality's [general portal terms](https://www.sofia.bg/terms-of-use), amended 2024-03-26, also note that individual documents can have additional terms; public accessibility alone is not an unrestricted reuse grant.
- OSRM explicitly supports walk mode through profiles. A future pedestrian implementation needs a walking-configured graph and the exact input snapshot; a car-profile response cannot be described as a walking route. No hosted endpoint, service response rights, route accuracy or accessibility was checked.

## Sourced facts versus inference

**Sourced facts:** the specific publishers, resources, declared dates and terms above were checked directly. GTFS validity dates, municipal as-of dates, catalog metadata dates and OSM cutoff dates have different meanings. The conflicting GTFS licence labels and unspecified municipal CC BY version are sourced limitations, not permissions inferred from the word “open”.

**Inference / implementation boundary:** these inputs could support area context, but that does not prove useful coverage, current services, walkability or permission for every use. The fixture adapter transforms supplied GTFS-shaped stop rows and accepts already-normalized schedules, municipal features and routes; it does not import GTFS ZIPs, join trips/calendars, convert geographic polygons to points or run OSRM. All fixtures remain invented and must not be presented as real Sofia observations.

## Provenance fields

`DataSourceProvenance` records `name`, `sourceUrl`, `datasetDate`, `checkedAt` and `reuseTerms`. A future real-data caller must identify the date's meaning (e.g. `valid from 2026-10-05` versus `as of 2026-08-28`), the exact source and the observed terms/limitations. Unknown dates or permissions stay explicitly unknown; `checkedAt` never substitutes for a missing dataset date. These documentation checks do not populate fixture provenance automatically. Synthetic tests use fake URLs and values and make no network requests; their documentation checks assert citations and evidence distinctions locally, not continued remote availability or legal clearance.

## Image use: facts and inference

**Observed source facts:** listing photos bear advertising-agency watermarks. The reviewed general terms and privacy page do not provide an image-use permission or an explicit third-party reuse licence. No API, data licence, or terms specifically authorizing automated use were found in the reviewed sources.

**Inference, not legal advice:** a watermark and absence of a permission grant do not establish who owns each image or what rights a particular advertiser has. Do not assume that publicly viewable images, the robots.txt policy, or the site's general copyright footer grants permission to copy, redistribute, publish, or send photos to an external provider. The actual rights holder may be the photographer, agency, advertiser, or another party; confirmation is needed.

## Restrictions and unresolved questions

- The reviewed sources do not answer whether bounded automated retrieval for private, local analysis is permitted, or specify access/rate conditions.
- No reviewed source grants permission to store, reproduce, distribute, or publicly display listing text or photographs.
- The privacy page's treatment of contact data makes personal-data minimization relevant; this project does not treat publicly displayed contact information as unrestricted for reuse.
- It remains unresolved who controls rights in individual listing photographs, whether analysis by a configured external vision provider is allowed, and what retention or attribution conditions apply.
- The empty robots.txt `Disallow` is a robots instruction, not a content licence, permission contract, or resolution of the site's challenge response.

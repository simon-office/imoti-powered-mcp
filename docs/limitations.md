# Product limitations and uncertainty

This tool provides evidence from listings observed during bounded searches and refreshes. It is not a complete catalogue or a verification service.

## Coverage and freshness

Searches are bounded by requested filters and page limits; results may be omitted, inaccessible, or changed between observations. A listing missing from a later search means **no longer observed**, not sold or unavailable on the market. Local history begins at the first observation and cannot establish earlier prices or events. Freshness depends on the last successful observation; cached information can be older, and a failed or blocked refresh does not make it current.

Asking prices are advertised prices, not transaction prices. Comparisons reflect only the available sample, its stated size and period, and may not be comparable in condition, terms, or exact location.

## Property and photo evidence

Listing fields and descriptions are publisher-provided and may be incomplete, stale, inaccurate, or contradictory. Photos show only selected views at a particular time. Hidden defects, structural condition, workmanship, legal status, and the true state of a property cannot be inferred from photos.

Render/photo classification relies on the host model or configured provider when available, and is uncertain. Deterministic PNG-signature detection alone does not detect JPEG renders; absence of that signal is not proof that an image is a photograph. Image readability and model findings have their own coverage and uncertainty limits.

## Location

Location may be approximate, missing, or ambiguous. Location precision can be limited to a street, neighbourhood, or unknown; a seller or agency office must not be mistaken for the property's location. Nearby-place and transit context is limited by the source data, its coverage, and its freshness. Distance estimates do not establish walking time or actual accessibility unless an explicitly identified routing source supports that claim.

Treat outputs as decision support, verify important facts with the advertiser and inspect the property in person.
